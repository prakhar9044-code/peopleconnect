## 🌌 Overview

**PeopleConnect** is a next-generation digital ecosystem engineered to seamlessly bridge the gap between social welfare initiatives and accessible mental wellness resources. 

Born from a vision of profound social impact by **Prakhar**, this platform translates empathy into a scalable, interactive web experience. Crafted with a strict focus on a **human-centric, cyber-wellness aesthetic**, the platform utilizes a deep, AMOLED-optimized dark mode infused with neon cyan and electric blue accents. This dark luxury design minimizes cognitive load and eye strain while maximizing engagement through fluid, physics-based interactions.

<br>

## ✨ Interactive Ecosystem Features

<details>
<summary><b>🧠 The Mental Wellness Hub</b> <i>(Click to Expand)</i></summary>

A secure, private sanctuary for accessing mental health resources.
- 🔮 **Predictive Tracking:** AI-driven insights to monitor your wellness journey over time.
- 👨‍⚕️ **Professional Directory:** Interactive geolocation-based connection to certified mental health experts.
- 🌙 **Sanctuary Mode UI:** Immerse yourself in a calming, low-light environment driven by Three.js particle backgrounds.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>🤝 Social Welfare Integration</b> <i>(Click to Expand)</i></summary>

A streamlined, high-performance dashboard connecting users with actionable community programs.
- 📡 **Live Campaign Radar:** Real-time, websocket-driven updates on local welfare programs.
- 🧩 **Volunteer Algorithm:** Smart matching algorithms connecting you to causes based on your interaction history.
- 🗺️ **Resource Mapping:** Visual, interactive maps charting local NGO and resource distribution.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>⚡ Spatial UI & High-Performance Architecture</b> <i>(Click to Expand)</i></summary>

Built without compromise, ensuring lightning-fast performance and cinematic interactions.
- 🧊 **Glassmorphism 2.0:** Deep frosted overlays on rich dark backgrounds.
- 🌀 **Three.js Spatial Elements:** Immersive 3D background canvases that react dynamically to user scrolling.
- 🌊 **GSAP Fluidity:** Silky-smooth, physics-based page transitions and micro-interactions.
</details>
